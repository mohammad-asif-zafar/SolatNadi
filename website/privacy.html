Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to SolatNadi ("we", "our", or "us"). SolatNadi is dedicated to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application operates with zero personal tracking and offline-first principles.

1. Information We Collect

SolatNadi does NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or device identifier.

2. Location Information

To calculate accurate Islamic prayer times and sunrise/sunset schedules, SolatNadi utilizes your selected city location or optional GPS coordinates:

  • Selected City: The city you select in the app is stored locally on your device database (SQLDelight/SQLite).
  • GPS Location (Optional): If enabled, GPS coordinates are processed on-device solely to query prayer calculation algorithms or fetch offline parameter data. Location data is never uploaded to any tracking server or shared with third parties.

3. Local Storage & Database

All application preferences (calculation methods, madhab preferences, time format, and prayer notification toggles) are stored locally on your device using a local SQLDelight SQLite database and platform preferences. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the application cache/data in your Android settings.

4. Notifications & Alarm Audio

SolatNadi uses local device exact alarms (via Android's AlarmManager) to trigger notifications and Azan audio (alarm_azan.mp3) at exact prayer times. These alarms execute entirely locally on your device without relying on remote push server tracking.

5. Third-Party Services

When online, the app queries the public Aladhan API to fetch monthly prayer timing parameters based on latitude/longitude and calculation method. No personal or user identification tokens are included in these API queries.

6. Children's Privacy

SolatNadi does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
