1. Introduction

Welcome to SolatNadi ("we", "our", or "us"). SolatNadi is dedicated to providing accurate, respectful Islamic prayer timing services with complete user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our offline-first data handling principles and how your data is treated when using the SolatNadi mobile application and website.

2. Information Collected

SolatNadi is engineered with a strict privacy-by-design architecture:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do NOT collect, transmit, track, or sell your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or account credentials.
  • No Account Required: SolatNadi functions completely without sign-in, user accounts, or registration.

3. How Information Is Used

Any technical data processed locally on your device (such as your chosen city or device timezone) is utilized solely to calculate accurate prayer times, update countdown timers, and schedule local Azan reminders.

4. Location Permissions

To provide accurate Islamic prayer schedules, SolatNadi processes location information in two ways:

  • User Selected City: When you manually choose a city, its latitude, longitude, and timezone are stored locally in your on-device SQLite database.
  • Device Location / GPS (Optional): If you enable automatic location detection, your GPS coordinates are accessed on-device solely to determine your location's prayer schedule parameters. Your location is never uploaded or shared with tracking servers.

5. Prayer Calculation Preferences

Your chosen calculation method (e.g. Muslim World League, ISNA, JAKIM), Juristic Method (Shafi'i or Hanafi), time format (12-hour or 24-hour), and visual theme settings are saved locally on your device via platform settings and local database storage.

6. Notifications & Exact Alarms

SolatNadi uses local device exact alarms (via Android's AlarmManager) to trigger Azan audio reminders (alarm_azan.mp3) at exact prayer times. These alarms execute 100% locally on your device without relying on external push notification servers.

7. Third-Party Services and APIs

When internet connectivity is available, the app queries the public Aladhan API to fetch raw prayer timing calculation data for your specified city coordinates and calculation method. No personal or device tracking tokens are transmitted in these requests.

8. Data Storage and Retention

SolatNadi uses an offline-first local database (SQLDelight). All prayer schedules, city lists, and user preference toggles remain stored on your physical device and persist until you choose to modify them or clear the app's cache.

9. Data Security

Because your preference data remains on your personal device and is not transmitted to external servers, the security of your data is backed by your device's native operating system security mechanisms.

10. Children's Privacy

SolatNadi does not collect any personal information from children or adults. The application is suitable and safe for users of all ages.

11. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have total control over your data. You can clear your location and notification preferences at any time by selecting a new city, toggling notification switches, or resetting the app data through your device Settings.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or legal requirements. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
